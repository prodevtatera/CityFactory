# Writing rules

- Quantitative outcome/cost claims must say whether they are measured, researched or assumed,
  include the measurement/source date, and link to evidence. Game design targets are proposals,
  not measurements. Do not present projected JevAstra savings as money actually saved.
- Name material exclusions and unchecked behavior. A passing test is not proof of deployment,
  user value, or production accuracy. Unknown charges are not zero.
- Keep dated evidence/history intact. Supersede an outdated claim with a dated correction;
  preserve the original where it explains a decision. Edit the relevant lines, not whole pages.
- [Open backlog](../../docs/backlog.md) contains open work only. Move completed items whole to
  [done](../../docs/backlog_done.md), preserving IDs, dates and corrections. Split remaining gaps
  into open items. Fix links when moving items.
- Link to a dated measurement rather than copying it across multiple documents.
- Preserve verbatim user quotations in their original language. Label generated examples and
  simulated feedback as such; never present them as independent user evidence.
- Use plain language and only enough structure to make the document easy to use.

Adapted from ContentMgmt's document and backlog workflow on 2026-09-18. Its video-production
rules and product-validation gates do not apply to CityFactory.
