WELCOME TO SYRUP VALLEY

Your factory, from the ground up.

Choose a module from the toolbox and click the land to place it. Build all seven essentials, then open your factory.

  1. Build. Roads bring trucks in. Utilities supply the whole plot. Place your production and support modules.
  2. Make. Supply trucks deliver ingredients and packaging. Follow the colored transfer paths; longer paths take longer. Each batch makes 100 cartons, and the order needs 300.
  3. Release. Release each completed batch. Trucks load up to 200 released cartons. Send a partial load or wait; only departed stock counts as delivered.

A simplified learning factory. One module of each kind, automatic internal transfers, one illustrative syrup recipe. Pause to move modules for free. Dock approaches must stay clear. Retry the same order to compare times. Cleaning and staffing are included in each module.

Mouse: click to place, right-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Keyboard: choose a tool, arrow keys to move, R to rotate, Enter to place, Escape to cancel. Space pauses when the scene is focused.
